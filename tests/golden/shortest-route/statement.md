# Shortest Route

There are `n` towns, numbered 1 to `n`, and `m` one-way roads. Road `i` goes from town
`a_i` to town `b_i` and takes `w_i` minutes; several roads may join the same two towns.
Print the fewest minutes needed to travel from town 1 to town `n`, or `-1` if town `n`
cannot be reached.

The first line holds `n` and `m`. Then `m` lines follow, each with `a_i b_i w_i`.

## Constraints

- 2 ≤ n ≤ 5·10^4
- 1 ≤ m ≤ 10^5
- 1 ≤ w_i ≤ 10^9
