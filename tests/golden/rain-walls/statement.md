# Rain Between Walls

`n` walls of width 1 stand in a row; wall `i` has height `h_i`. After heavy rain, water
fills every gap up to the lower of the highest walls on its left and on its right. Print how
many units of water stay between the walls.

The first line holds `n`. The second line holds the heights.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
- 0 ≤ h_i ≤ 10^9
