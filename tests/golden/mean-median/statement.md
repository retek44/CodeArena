# Mean and Median

Read `n` integers and print two numbers on one line: their mean and their median. When `n`
is even, the median is the mean of the two middle values in sorted order.

The first line holds `n`. The second line holds the integers. Answers within an absolute or
relative error of 10^-6 are accepted.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
- |x_i| ≤ 10^6
