# Buy Low Sell High

You know the price of a share on each of `n` days. Buy one share on one day and sell it on a
later day. Print the largest profit you can make, or 0 if every sale would lose money.

The first line holds `n`. The second line holds the prices `p_1 … p_n`.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
- 1 ≤ p_i ≤ 10^9
